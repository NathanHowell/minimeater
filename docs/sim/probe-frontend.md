## Settling after a probe is plugged in (bias_at_tip)

Time for the reading to stay within 0.01 F of its final value, node starting at VREF (empty jack). The RC is the 47k bias against 100 nF.

| Probe | 32 F | 225 F | 500 F |
|---|---|---|---|
| ThermoWorks Pro-Series | 34 ms | 6 ms | 2 ms |
| Maverick ET-732 | 37 ms | 20 ms | 3 ms |
| Generic 100k B3950 | 34 ms | 7 ms | 2 ms |

One 20 SPS conversion is 50 ms, so the first reading after a plug-in can still be settling. Firmware should drop the first conversion after a probe appears (to check against `drivers::ads122c04`).

## Ripple on 3V3A

Gain from 3V3A to REFP (the filtered VREF_PROBE) and to the ADC pin, 100 k probe (about 25 C), FB2 modelled as 0.2 ohm + 0.8 uH || 700 ohm [est].

| Frequency | to REFP | to ADC pin |
|---|---|---|
| 60 Hz | -0.0 dB | -10.6 dB |
| 1,000 Hz | -1.5 dB | -31.2 dB |
| 10,000 Hz | -16.3 dB | -66.0 dB |
| 100,000 Hz | -36.2 dB | -105.9 dB |
| 256,000 Hz | -44.4 dB | -122.3 dB |
| 1,500,000 Hz | -61.7 dB | -154.8 dB |

A 20 mVpp 1.5 MHz buck ripple (assumed) is 17 uV at REFP. The ADS122C04's average input current of 5 nA (datasheet) at its 256 kHz modulator rate is a charge of 20 fC per cycle, 0.2 uV on the 100 nF node.
