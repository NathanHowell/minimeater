In current limit a TPS2553 is a constant-current source (no foldback), so a stalled servo (4-5 ohm across a 0.6 A limit) leaves 1.1-1.5 W in a SOT-23 whose theta would take it to 300 C: the thermal shutdown ends it, by design (ADR 0004). What the datasheet does not give is how many seconds that takes to reach 135 C on this board, nor how warm the board gets in the three seconds before the firmware latches the fault; that goes into the bring-up list (`.3.9`).

## Switch-on and surge

- **U12 soft start:** it carries C dV/dt = 505 mA into 120 uF of VBUS_F at 4.2 V/ms for 1.2 ms while its drop falls from 5 V to nothing: 1.5 mJ, 1.3 W average for that millisecond, then 0.05 W. The datasheet's thermal-shutdown time at 85 C is over a second at 1.3 W (Figure 26 `[fig]`), against a pulse of 1.2 ms.
- **D1 in a hot plug** (the 18 corners of `power.md`, 0.25-2 uH and 0.05-0.2 ohm of cable, nominal and derated caps): at most 0.8 A and 3 uJ against the SMF5.0A's 200 W / 21.7 A pulse rating `[est]`, so D1 sees nothing like its rating at plug-in.

## Other limits met

- **L1:** peak current at the TX burst is 0.35 A + half the ripple, 0.17 A at nominal L, so 0.52 A against Isat 1.8 A `[est]`. U4's high-side limit (3 A) is above that Isat, so a hard short on 3V3 drives L1 into saturation until the limit or U12 (2.04 A in) steps in; nothing the board does in normal running comes near it.
- **Power banks:** the cook case draws 90 mA at VBUS_RAW, 0.45 W. ADR 0004 put it at about 120 mA and worried about banks that cut off under 50-100 mA; that is at the top of the band where banks may cut off, so the question stands. The keep-alive footprint ADR 0004 allows for stays open for bring-up (`.3.9`), when a real bank can be tried.
