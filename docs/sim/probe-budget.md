# Probe channel error budget

Generated by `uv run minimeater-sim` (`src/minimeater/sim/budget.py`). One channel as on the schematic (47k bias at the tip, 1k to the ADC pin, 100 nF), every board-side error source at its datasheet limit, the board at 85 C. The DC solution is closed form, checked against ngspice to 1e-8 in the ratio. The probe's own tolerance, lead resistance and a damp probe's leakage are not the board's and are left out (ADR 0002, `docs/modeling/probes.md`).

## Sampling kickback (transient)

The ADC's input as a switched capacitor at the 256 kHz modulator rate, emptied to AVSS every cycle (the worst case), on the generic 100k probe at 225 F; the reading is the mean of v(ain) over a 50 ms conversion. The datasheet gives no sampling capacitance; the first row is the capacitance whose average current is the spec's 5 nA at full scale, the second ten times that.

| C sample | Average current | Per-cycle dip | Reading error | DC current of the same average |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.9 fF | 0.54 nA | 21.1 nV | +0.0007 F | +0.0007 F |
| 59.2 fF | 5.41 nA | 211.4 nV | +0.0067 F | +0.0068 F |

The 100 nF node takes each kick without a visible step (the per-cycle charge is fC against 100 nF), and the reading moves exactly as a DC current of the same average would: the kickback is the datasheet's input current, already in the budget. Each jack has its own 100 nF, so the mux switching between jacks needs no settling either; plug-in settling is in `probe-frontend.md`.

## Reading it

- In the pit and food ranges the bias resistor dominates (tolerance and drift), then the ADC's gain error. None of it is worth a part change: a 0.05 % or 10 ppm/K resistor would take a tenth of a degree off a budget that already passes with margin.
- Above 400 F the 100k probes are a few hundred ohms and the ratio is small, so the ADC's INL takes over: up to about 3 F at 600 F worst case. That is outside the control range (ADR 0002).
- The 1M Maverick at 32 F is 2 F worst case, mostly gain error at a ratio near 1; it is a food-probe ice-bath check, where the power-up ambient self-check and the per-probe offset trim (ADR 0002) apply.
- The input-current terms (ESD and ADC) together are under 0.05 F in the bands; ADR 0002's 30 nA budget was conservative. The TPD4E05U06's 10 nA is its max over temperature, so the 'verify at 60 C' item is closed.
