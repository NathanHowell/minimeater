# 5 V input in ngspice

Generated by `uv run minimeat-sim` (`src/minimeat/sim/power.py`). USB source 5.25 V behind the cable's L and R, D1 SMF5V0A (6.8 V breakdown, 0.11 ohm), F1 PTC 0.15 ohm [est], then C1 100 uF, C2 and C4 10 uF, 3 x 100 nF with 3 mohm ESR. `derated` is the MLCC DC-bias loss at 5 V [est]: 45 / 55 / 55 / 90 %. The PTC and any cable resistance damp the ringing; the TVS never conducts. The corner case (long, low-resistance cable, derated caps) reaches the 6 V limit. The current spike is the cost of 100 uF behind a 0.2 ohm path: about 0.9 mC drawn in tens of microseconds, against USB's 50 uC inrush guideline. A host with a fast over-current trip can see that as a fault; check on a scope at bring-up, and if ports trip, drop C1 to 22 uF.
