# 5 V input in ngspice

Generated by `uv run minimeater-sim` (`src/minimeater/sim/power.py`). USB source 5.25 V behind the cable's L and R, D1 SMF5V0A (6.8 V breakdown, 0.11 ohm) and C41 100 nF on VBUS_RAW, then U12 TPS259531 eFuse (behavioural: 34 mohm, 2.04 A limit from R35 1k, 4.2 V/ms soft start from C42 10 nF, 5.7 V output clamp, 0.4 ms turn-on delay [est]) into VBUS_F: C1 100 uF, C2 and C4 10 uF, 3 x 100 nF with 3 mohm ESR. `derated` is the MLCC DC-bias loss at 5 V [est]: 45 / 55 / 55 / 90 %. Absolute maximum at VBUS_F: 6 V on the TLV62569 (SLVSDG1C) and the TLV75533P (VIN), 7 V on the TPS2553 (IN, OUT); 20 V at the eFuse's input (SLVSE57C). The first board's PTC (F1) is the `ptc` column, for comparison.

With the PTC, the board's 100 uF sat straight on the cable: a long, thick cable rang VBUS_F to 6.6-7.2 V, over the 6 V parts, and every plug-in drew 15-40 A and 0.3-0.6 mC in tens of microseconds, against USB's 50 uC inrush guideline (minimeat-kqt, minimeat-brt). The eFuse fixes both. At attach the cable sees only the 100 nF input caps (a couple of uC); it rings the input to about 7 V, where D1 clamps it, far under the eFuse's 20 V. VBUS_F then rises at the soft-start rate, about half an amp into the board's capacitance for a millisecond, and never above 5.3 V. More input capacitance (1-2 uF) only puts more energy into the TVS, so the input keeps just the datasheet's 0.1 uF (C41, at the IN pins).

UVLO: R33 100k over R34 43.2k puts EN at 3.98 V rising, 3.65 V falling (1.2/1.1 V thresholds), and keeps the EN pin under its 7 V rating while the input rings. FLT is not used: when the eFuse trips, the board is off.

## 3V3 buck (TLV62569, checked against the datasheet, not simulated)

- L1 2.2 uH with 3 x 10 uF at the output is the datasheet's standard LC (Table 4, 10 uF at 2.2 uH, also proven at 22 and 2 x 22 uF); with the DC-bias loss at 3.3 V (about 55 %) and the -50 % tolerance the effective output capacitance is still above the 10 uF row.
- R3 453k / R4 100k with C7 6.8 pF: 3.32 V, and the 6.8 pF feed-forward is the datasheet's value for R2 = 100k (R2 maximum 200k).
- Switching 1.5 MHz, high-side current limit 3 A, soft start 800 us, UVLO 2.3-2.45 V falling: the servo turn-on above leaves the rail at 4.5 V, far from UVLO.
- There is no vendor model of the buck for ngspice: `rails.md` runs the 5 V and 3V3 transients with a behavioural one (constant-power input, the datasheet's own load-step capture at the output); measure the 3V3 ripple and a Wi-Fi burst at bring-up.
