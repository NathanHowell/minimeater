# 5 V input in ngspice

Generated by `uv run minimeat-sim` (`src/minimeat/sim/power.py`). USB source 5.25 V behind the cable's L and R, D1 SMF5V0A (6.8 V breakdown, 0.11 ohm), F1 PTC 0.15 ohm [est], then C1 100 uF, C2 and C4 10 uF, 3 x 100 nF with 3 mohm ESR. `derated` is the MLCC DC-bias loss at 5 V [est]: 45 / 55 / 55 / 90 %. Absolute maximum at VBUS_F: 6 V on the TLV62569 (datasheet SLVSDG1C, VIN); the TLV75533 and TPS2553 figures are still to confirm.

The PTC and any cable resistance damp the ringing; the TVS never conducts. The corner case (long, low-resistance cable, derated caps) reaches the 6 V limit. The current spike is the cost of 100 uF behind a 0.2 ohm path: about 0.9 mC drawn in tens of microseconds, against USB's 50 uC inrush guideline. A host with a fast over-current trip can see that as a fault; check on a scope at bring-up, and if ports trip, drop C1 to 22 uF.

The rail stays above 4.5 V. The source current is 0.9 A for about a millisecond, over a Default-class (500 mA) port's limit: firmware should enable the servo only on a 1.5 A or 3 A source, or while the fan and radio are quiet (ICD: loads are limited by source class).

## 3V3 buck (TLV62569, checked against the datasheet, not simulated)

- L1 2.2 uH with 3 x 10 uF at the output is the datasheet's standard LC (Table 4, 10 uF at 2.2 uH, also proven at 22 and 2 x 22 uF); with the DC-bias loss at 3.3 V (about 55 %) and the -50 % tolerance the effective output capacitance is still above the 10 uF row.
- R3 453k / R4 100k with C7 6.8 pF: 3.32 V, and the 6.8 pF feed-forward is the datasheet's value for R2 = 100k (R2 maximum 200k).
- Switching 1.5 MHz, high-side current limit 3 A, soft start 800 us, UVLO 2.3-2.45 V falling: the servo turn-on above leaves the rail at 4.5 V, far from UVLO.
- A load-step simulation would need a vendor model; measure the 3V3 ripple and a Wi-Fi burst at bring-up.
