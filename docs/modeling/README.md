# Modeling

- [`bge-large.md`](bge-large.md) is generated by `uv run minimeat-model`. It covers airflow, pressure drop, fans and the baseline dynamics.
- [`control.md`](control.md) is generated by `uv run minimeat-control`. It is the controller bake-off.
- Source: `src/minimeat/model/`. Tests: `tests/`.
- This page holds the hand-written conclusions. Re-check them whenever the model or its parameters change.

**Scope.** Big Green Egg Large, pit 150F (chili drying) to 325F (hot-and-fast brisket). Grilling is out of scope: remove the unit and use the manual draft settings.

## Conclusions (2026-10-11, model v0, mostly estimated parameters)

### 1. The egg needs very little air in steady state
| Pit temp | Air needed |
|---|---|
| 150F | ~0.3 CFM (20C calm) to ~0.8 CFM (-10C, windy) |
| 225F | ~0.6–1.25 CFM |
| 325F | ~1.2–2.0 CFM |

- The parameter uncertainty band spans about 0.8–2x these numbers.
- The fan matters mainly for transients: light-off, lid recovery and setpoint increases.
- At 150F the fire is only about 300 W, so it may go out. Fire-out detection is needed.

### 2. Our hardware is the restriction, not the egg
Once the daisy wheel is a quarter open or more, most of the pressure drop is in the 1.375" (31.6 mm ID) intake tube and our own box. At 6 CFM the tube and box alone need about 28 Pa (2.9 mmH2O), which exceeds every Noctua fan's shut-off pressure. Every candidate therefore delivers only 3–5 CFM.

### 3. Fan: Noctua NF-A6x25 5V PWM, with the NF-A8 as backup
- **Flow:** about 4.5 CFM delivered.
- **Holding 325F:** reachable with nominal parameters at modest speed.
- **Worst case (winter, high-loss parameters):** the maximum steady temperature is about 318–334F, which is marginal.
- **NF-A8 5V PWM:** about 340–356F in the same worst case, for a box 20 mm bigger.

**A stopped axial fan barely obstructs the egg's convective draft.** With the flap open and the fan stopped, about 0.9–1.4 CFM flows. That covers most of the 150–325F range with only the flap moving, so the fan is silent most of the time.

**Radial blowers are out.**
- A stopped radial blower chokes natural draft to about 0.35–0.55 CFM, so it would have to run even at low-and-slow.
- They are also about 20 dB louder.

### 4. Keep the box's internal losses low
k_box is the loss coefficient of everything inside the box between the fan and the tube (transition, open flap, inlet grille), referenced to tube velocity.

| k_box | NF-A6x25 delivered flow |
|---|---|
| 0.5 | 5.4 CFM |
| 2 | 4.5 CFM |
| 8 | 3.0 CFM |

**Design target:** k_box ≤ 1.5. CFD (`.2.13`) is where this gets optimised.

### 5. The damper is the main actuator, and it must seal
- **It must be proportional.** An on/off flap limit-cycles, because natural draft through an open flap exceeds the requirement.
- **Leak requirement: equivalent closed-flap leak ≤ 0.3 cm².**
  - At 150F, 0.5 cm² leaks about 0.08 CFM, roughly 25% of the requirement.
  - At 0.3 cm² it is about 0.05 CFM, roughly 16%.

### 6. Control: offset-free MPC on airflow, with FF+PI as a simpler fallback
Full results are in [`control.md`](control.md): 4 scenarios × 3 egg parameter sets, 0.3F probe noise. Each controller is given a deliberately imperfect firmware-side model, and has to cope with the wrong top-vent setting and the wrong egg parameters.

| Controller | Light-off overshoot | Hold RMS | Lid recovery | Post-lid overshoot | Servo strokes/h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HeaterMeter-style PI on % | 43F (worst 67) | 3.6F | 3.6 min | 60F (worst 98) | 5.5 |
| FF + PI on airflow | 13F (worst 37) | 1.3F | 5.9 min | 9F | 1.7 |
| **MPC on airflow** | **5F (worst 7)** | 1.4F | 6.0 min | **5F (worst 11)** | 1.9 |

The recommended controller is built from the parts below. Each piece is cheap on an ESP32-C6.

**1. Command airflow (CFM), not %.** An actuator-inversion table maps airflow to flap angle and fan speed. It comes from the flow model and gets refined by calibration. This removes the main nonlinearity: plant gain differs between the flap-only and fan regions.
  - Fan speeds below the minimum are produced by cycling the fan on and off.

**2. Feedforward.** The steady-state airflow needed for the setpoint, computed from the model and the on-board ambient sensor.

**3. Autotune by step-response system identification, not a relay test.**
  - A 30% airflow step at the setpoint, fitted to a first-order-plus-dead-time model.
  - Identified values: about 50–160 F per CFM, a time constant of about 7–9 min, and 100–130 s of dead time.
  - The gain changes with setpoint and egg, so models are scheduled by setpoint.

**4. Offset-free linear MPC.**
  - Inputs: the identified model plus a Kalman disturbance estimate. The disturbance estimate absorbs fuel, weather, food load and model error.
  - Horizon: 30 min in 6 blocks, about 6 decision variables, with box constraints.
  - Cost: overshoot costs 8x undershoot, because a kamado cannot be actively cooled.
  - Solving it takes milliseconds every 10 s with projected gradient.

**5. Reference shaping during light-off and setpoint increases only.** The reference approaches the setpoint at a capped rate and is tethered to the pit temperature, so a slow-spreading fire can't wind the controller up. It disarms once the pit reaches setpoint, so disturbances see the true setpoint.

**6. Lid recovery.** After the lid closes, replay 1.5x the pre-lid airflow until the pit is within 20F of setpoint. The ceramic is still hot, so the pre-lid airflow is nearly right.
  - Feedback alone either flares the fire (the baseline overshoots by 60–98F) or, with a model that ignores the hot walls, recovers in 30–50 min.
  - The boost factor sets recovery time against overshoot:

| Boost | Recovery | Post-lid overshoot |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0x | ~12 min | ~1–5F |
| 1.5x | ~6 min | ~5–9F |
| 2.0x | ~5 min | ~12–16F |
| 3.0x | ~4 min | ~25–30F |

**The trade-off for slower overshoot-free light-off:** MPC takes 20–30 min to reach setpoint versus about 10 min for the baseline. On a kamado that is the right trade, because overshoot takes hours to bleed off (the ceramic shell's time constant is about 1.5 h).

**FF+PI is nearly as good apart from startup overshoot at 325F.** It's simpler, so it's the fallback if MPC proves fragile on real hardware.

### 7. Measurements that would replace the biggest estimates
1. **Intake tube:** OD and ID with calipers. The forum prior is 1.375" OD.
2. **Daisy wheel:** open areas at the user's usual settings.
3. **Natural-draft calibration cook:**
   - Weigh the lump before and after.
   - Log dome, outer shell and ambient temperatures.
4. **System curve on the egg** (`.2.10`).
5. **Fan bake-off P-Q and noise curves** (`.2.11`).
6. **A real-egg step test:** validates the identified model structure and the controller ranking.
