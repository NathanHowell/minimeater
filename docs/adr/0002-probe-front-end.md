# ADR 0002: Temperature probe front-end

- Status: accepted (2026-10-11), amended 2026-10-11: the ADC is the ADS122C04 on I2C, not the ADS1220 on SPI (see the amendment below)
- Bead: `minimeat-seq.1.5`
- Evidence: [`docs/modeling/probes.md`](../modeling/probes.md), generated by `uv run minimeat-probes` from `src/minimeat/model/probes.py`
- Constraint: the PCB is assembled by JLCPCB, so every PCB part must be in JLC stock. Volume is about 5 boards. Probes are bought elsewhere.

## Context

The box needs:
- 4 interchangeable 2.5 mm thermistor jacks. Any jack can take the pit probe or a food probe.
- An optional K-type thermocouple for the pit.
- An outside-air (ambient) sensor for the controller's feedforward.

Probes in use vary:
- **ThermoWorks Pro-Series and generic import probes:** about 100k at 25C, falling to about 200-300 ohm at 500F.
- **Legacy Maverick ET-732:** about 1 M at 25C.

The control range is a 150-325F pit. Food probes also see 32F during an ice-bath check. Firmware acceptance is ±2F against a reference at ambient and boiling (`.4.7`).

## Decision

**Part policy:** where options exist, pick the higher-quality part unless the price gap is large.

**ADC: TI ADS1220IPWR** (JLC C48263, about 15k in stock, $2.46). Configuration:
- **Measurement:** ratiometric, PGA bypassed, 4 single-ended inputs (AIN0-3 against AVSS). External reference on REFP0/REFN0, taken from the same filtered `VREF_PROBE` rail that feeds the dividers.
- **Bus:** SPI mode 1, with DRDY routed to a GPIO.
- **Conversions:** single-shot at 20 SPS with simultaneous 50/60 Hz rejection (`50/60 = 01b`), round-robin over 4 channels. Average 8 conversions per channel, giving one reading per channel about every 1.6 s. The control period is 10 s.
- **Housekeeping:** every pass also reads the internal temperature sensor (board health) and does an offset measurement (inputs shorted to mid-supply, MUX `1110b`).
- **Why the ADS1220 over the ADS1120:** the ADS1120IPWR (C84284, 16-bit) models within 0.03F of the ADS1220 at our bias. It costs more at JLC today ($2.74), so there is no reason to pick it. If the ADS1220 goes out of stock, the ADS1120 is the fallback; confirm pin compatibility first.
- **The ESP32-C6 internal SAR ADC is rejected.** Its reference isn't ratiometric and its INL is about ±10 LSB. That gives a modeled worst case of 40-100F at the cold and hot ends, and still several F at mid-range.

**Bias resistor: 47.0k, 0.1%, 25 ppm/K, 0603, from `VREF_PROBE` to each tip.** Vishay TNPW060347K0BEEA (C2089026, thin film, about 19k in stock, $0.09). Quality over price per project policy. Alternate: YAGEO RT0603BRD0747KL (C326725).

| Probe family | Worst design-attributable error, 32-500F | Pit range 150-325F |
|---|---|---|
| Mainstream 100k-class | ≤ 0.6F | ≤ 0.25F |
| 1M-class Maverick | 0.8F at 100F, 3.7F at 32F | ≤ 0.4F |

  - **Noise is irrelevant at every bias:** about 0.001F at 225F. The error comes from ADC gain and offset, input current and bias tolerance, so resolution does not drive the part choice.
  - **The sweep's numerical optimum (150-220k) was rejected.** It only helps the Maverick at freezing. In exchange it doubles the 500F error on mainstream probes and raises the source impedance, which makes RF pickup and leakage sensitivity worse. 47k sits on the flat part of the curve for every family.
  - **Self-heating is at most 0.1F.** No switched excitation is needed.

**Per-input network**, in order from jack to ADC:
1. Jack tip.
2. ESD: one **TPD4E05U06DQAR** (C138714, 4 channels, 0.5 pF) at the jacks covers all four tips. Its leakage is counted in the 30 nA input-current budget; verify the max leakage at 60C when picking the part.
3. 1k series resistor.
4. Node with the 47k bias resistor up to `VREF_PROBE`.
5. 100 nF X7R to AGND at the ADC pin. Ratiometric DC measurement makes capacitor tolerance irrelevant. Each node has its own capacitor, so mux switching has no settling problem; the node time constant is under 5 ms.
6. The jack sleeve and ring both go to AGND, so a stereo jack accepts the mono probe plugs.

**`VREF_PROBE`:** 3V3 through a ferrite bead and 10 Ω, with 10 µF + 100 nF. It feeds only the four dividers and REFP0. The reference input current is ±10 nA. The rail's absolute value is irrelevant.

**Jacks: PJ-242-class 2.5 mm SMD** (SHOU HAN PJ-242, C431536, about 20k in stock, $0.21). The LCSC datasheet link didn't return a PDF, so pinout and switch contacts are not yet verified.
- If the chosen footprint has a normally-closed tip switch, wire the switch to AGND. An empty jack then reads as a hard short, which separates "unplugged" from "probe broken (open)".
- Without a switch, ratio thresholds still work:

| Reading | Ratio x |
|---|---|
| Short / unplugged-with-switch | below 2e-4 |
| Valid probe, 700F to -20F | 1.3e-3 to 0.9985 |
| Open (no probe, or broken probe) | above 0.9995 |

**Firmware conversion:**
- Per-probe Steinhart-Hart coefficients (A, B, C), selected by probe type, with presets for the three families in `probes.py`.
- An optional per-probe offset trim.
- **Power-up self-check:** before light-off, every connected probe should read the ambient sensor ±2F. A probe that disagrees is flagged as a wrong type or faulty. This is cheap, because the ambient sensor is accurate (below).

**Ambient (outside air): TI TMP117AIDRVR** (C699536, about 29k in stock, $1.02) on I2C.
- Loop sensitivity is about 0.5F of transient pit offset per 1C of ambient error, which the integrator removes. A 2C sensor would do for control.
- The TMP117's ±0.1C pays for the probe self-check above, at the price of a TMP102.
- **Placement dominates accuracy:** an isolated board tongue at the box inlet grille, upstream of everything, with no copper pour and away from the regulators, the fan MOSFET and the module (`.3.10`).

**Thermocouple (optional, DNP): MAX31856MUD+T** (C2653162, about 4.5k in stock, $4.34) on the same SPI bus with its own CS.
- Its thermistor-equivalent range isn't needed for 150-325F. It stays as a footprint for anyone who wants a faster, rugged pit sensor.
- Mini-K connectors are not stocked at JLC. The footprint is a 2-pin pad pair for a hand-fitted pigtail.

## Consequences

- **Pins:** SPI (SCLK, MOSI, MISO) + CS_ADC + DRDY + CS_TC = 6 GPIO. I2C adds 2 GPIO (TMP117 ALERT not used).
- **Accuracy is set by the probes, not the board.** Thermistor interchangeability (often ±1-2F) and a damp probe's insulation leakage (5F at 32F for a 1M probe with a 20 M shunt) dominate. That is why the firmware gets per-probe coefficients and the ambient self-check rather than a better ADC.
- **ADC heat sensitivity:** the ADS1220's input current rises with temperature (about 5 nA at 25C, 20-50 nA at 85C). The budget assumes 30 nA at a hot board, and the board must stay below 85C. The egg's outer shell runs 65-90C at 225-325F (model), so box placement and venting matter. This is recorded in the ICD (`.1.2`).
- **Re-run point:** if the probe set changes (e.g. ThermoWorks lines with different curves), add the family to `PROBES` and re-run `uv run minimeat-probes`. The bias choice is insensitive within the 100k class.

## Verify at schematic or bring-up

- ~~PJ-242 pinout and whether its tip switch exists~~ Resolved from the manufacturer drawing (SHOU HAN spec sheet, wmsc.lcsc.com copy of C431536): six SMD pads plus two Ø1.3 positioning holes; pad 6 is the tip, pad 7 its normally-closed switch contact, pads 2, 3 and 4 the sleeve and ring contacts, pad 5 the ring switch. A mono probe plug's sleeve touches 2, 3 and 4. Wiring: 6 to the probe node, 7 to GND (an empty jack reads as a short), 2, 3 and 4 to GND, 5 unused. Which of pads 2/3/4 is the sleeve rather than a ring is read from the drawing's plug figure and should be confirmed with a plug on the first board.
- TPD4E05U06 leakage at 60C.
- ADS1220 offset and gain against a 0.1% resistor decade (`.3.12` SPICE / bring-up).
- Steinhart-Hart coefficients for the user's actual probes: ice bath, boiling water, and a third point.

## Amendment (2026-10-11): ADS122C04 on I2C, no K-type input

The ADC is now the **TI ADS122C04IPWR** (JLC C2872338, 4.5k in stock, $7.92) on the same I2C bus as the TMP117. It is the I2C variant of the same converter, so nothing in the measurement design above changes: ratiometric against the external reference, PGA bypassed, 4 single-ended inputs, single shot at 20 SPS.

- **Why:** it removes SCLK, MOSI, MISO, the ADC chip select and the thermocouple chip select, five pins, for $5.5 more per board (about $27 for five). One bus, one set of pull-ups.
- **Checked in the datasheet (SBAS751B):**
  - Same analog core: 24 bit, 2.048 V internal reference, external REFP/REFN, dual matched IDACs, burn-out sources and an internal temperature sensor.
  - Input current with the PGA bypassed is +/-5 nA in normal mode and +/-10 nA in turbo mode, drifting 10 pA/C. At 85 C that is about 6 nA, far inside the 30 nA the error budget assumes.
  - -40 to 125 C ambient; supply 2.3 to 5.5 V.
  - I2C up to 1 MHz, 16 addresses from A0/A1; the board uses A1 = A0 = DGND, 0x40 (the TMP117 is 0x48).
- **Differences from the ADS1220 that matter:**
  - There is no 50/60 Hz setting. The 20 SPS rate rejects both together by design.
  - WREG writes one register per command byte, so a configuration is a run of command and data pairs in one frame.
  - The register fields are otherwise the same (MUX, GAIN, PGA_BYPASS, DR, TS, VREF), with VREF = 01b for the external reference.
- **Thermocouple input dropped.** The optional MAX31856 (SPI only) is gone with SPI. A K-type pit probe would need two ADC inputs, and all four are used by the thermistor jacks. The ADS122C04 can measure a thermocouple itself, so a later revision could spend two jacks or add a second ADC; nothing is reserved on this board.
- **The ADS1220/ADS1120 fallback** in the decision above no longer applies; the SPI driver is removed from the firmware.
- **Firmware:** `minimeat-app::drivers::ads122c04`, tested on the host against a scripted I2C bus. The board binding shares one bus between the ADC and the TMP117.
